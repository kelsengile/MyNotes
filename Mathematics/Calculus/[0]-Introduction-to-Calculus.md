# Introduction to Calculus

This note covers the essentials of calculus — limits, derivatives, and integrals — along with a look at where calculus shows up directly in computer science, especially optimization and machine learning.

## Why Calculus Matters
Calculus is the mathematics of change and accumulation. In CS, it shows up whenever something needs to be optimized — tuning a model's parameters, minimizing an error function, or understanding how a system evolves over time. You don't need to master every technique to use it well; you need a solid intuition for what a derivative and an integral actually represent.
