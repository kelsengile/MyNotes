# Introduction to Discrete Mathematics

This note covers discrete math — the branch of mathematics dealing with countable, distinct values rather than continuous ones. It's arguably the most directly applicable math for computer science, underpinning logic, proofs, graph theory, and the analysis of algorithms.

## Why Discrete Mathematics Matters
Computers work with discrete values — bits, integers, finite sets — not the continuous quantities calculus is built for. Discrete math gives you the tools to reason precisely about logic and correctness, count possibilities, and model relationships as graphs, all of which come up constantly in algorithms, databases, cryptography, and proofs of correctness.
